# Storey: full review, 31 October 2026

Five reviews ran on HEAD `fba8b3b`: UX on desktop, iPhone, visual design, code simplicity and performance. A verifier then tried to refute each review, and only the findings that survived are listed here. Screenshots were taken at 390×844, 820×1180, 1180×820, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. The house is "listing 91770873".

Owner's decisions for this pass:
- **Scope:** review, then build the quick wins. Big redesigns come back as proposals.
- **Phone:** for showing, walking and measuring, plus light Decorate and Furnish edits. Build, Electrics, Garden and Plot are view-only there.
- **Devices that matter:** iPhone and laptop/desktop. iPad keeps working but isn't optimised for.
- **Look:** a full-bleed canvas with floating chrome on every device, and the Glass look removed.

## Verdict

| | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Simple | 6.5 | Seven modes and six floating groups. Compare is split from the layout switch, and exports are spread over four places. |
| Intuitive | 6 | Picking a room in the default 3D view selects it out of sight under the roof. In Decorate the roof swallows the click. |
| Screen space | desktop 7, phone 4 | Desktop loses a solid 48 px bar. On the phone about 67 % of the screen is clear canvas, and the plan fills about 20 % of the screen. |
| Sexy | 6 | The brand is sound. The camera sits too high, white neighbours outshine the house, the plan shows its labels twice, and the CSS uses 20 font sizes. |
| Code | good | `npm run check` passes (1,650 tests, 34 s), and the pure logic in `lib` is the strongest part. Still missing: lint and CI, and about 30 raw number inputs that clamp as you type. |
| Performance | phone poor | Quality defaults to High everywhere. On a 3× phone a frame takes 50.6 ms on High and 7.5 ms on Low (SwiftShader). The first 3D view also downloads the path tracer. |

## What's wrong (verified)

**UX (desktop)**
- In the default 3D view a picked room is out of sight, and in Decorate the roof eats the click. *(UX1)*
- Compare sits in a dock popover with a "Single" option, away from As listed / Proposed. *(UX2)*
- "View" and "Views" sit side by side in the dock and mean different things. *(UX5)*
- The coach strip repeats the inspector's first line, covers the house, and only goes when you close it. *(UX9)*
- The Welcome screen shows a live mode bar that does nothing yet. *(UX8)*
- The Build card in 3D:
  - shows a dead "draw with R" hint *(UX7)*
  - gives the ground floor's area, not the whole house's *(UX7)*
  - has calibration and stairs rows that belong on the plan *(UX13)*
- Snapshot and Render live at the bottom of the Review page, which covers the 3D view they capture. *(UX4)*

**Phone**
- The chrome stacks up: top bar 48, pill, view cube, measure, dock 56, sheet peek 64, and 7 tabs at 56 plus the safe area.
- The phone plan fit leaves 78 px on the left and 132 px at the bottom, so the house is about 290×235 px. *(PH4)*
- Garden and Plot fit the whole street onto the phone screen. *(PH13)*
- The view-only decision isn't built: doors, windows, drags, Add point and the garden fields all still edit. *(PH3)*
- Every tap raises the sheet to half height, even just to read a room's name. *(PH11)*
- With nothing selected, the sheet's peek repeats the mode name. *(VIS6)*
- The view cube's targets are about 17 px; dock buttons are 36 px and segments about 30 px. *(PH6, PH7)*
- The phone shows things that don't apply there: Keyboard shortcuts, the palette button, Canvas Quiet/Full and plan-image tracing.

**Visual**
- The full-bleed layout exists only behind the Glass setting, so every device keeps a solid top bar. *(VIS1)*
- Plan labels are doubled: the traced listing image (opacity 0.3 by default) shows its own names under ours. *(VIS3)*
- The home camera looks down at 35°, so the sky never shows. Near-white neighbour blocks outshine the house. *(VIS4, VIS5)*
- The inspector card covers the measure button. *(VIS8)*
- Edge chips get clipped, and plan labels carry ft². *(VIS9)*
- Token leaks:
  - `var(--mono)` is undefined *(VIS11)*
  - about 78 raw hex values *(VIS11)*
  - 5 one-off shadows *(VIS11)*
  - 20 font sizes *(VIS12)*
  - brand.md is out of date *(VIS15)*

**Code**
- Bugs:
  - About 30 raw number inputs clamp on every keystroke. A pitch of 45 becomes 55, and you can't type 0.8. *(CODE1)*
  - The Furnish "place" event uses a stale rotation. *(CODE10)*
  - Long-press waits 550 ms in 3D and 500 ms on the plan. *(CODE9)*
- Simplicity:
  - App passes the same 6 view props to 4 viewers and re-renders on every drag frame. *(CODE4)*
  - The Glass code is spread over 10 places. *(CODE5)*
  - One dead file and about 11 dead exports. *(CODE11)*
  - Six unused CSS selectors. *(CODE11)*
  - Inspectors.tsx (1,508 lines) mixes six inspectors. *(CODE14)*
- Tooling: no ESLint, no CI, and `server/` and `scripts/` are never typechecked. *(CODE2)*

**Performance**
- Quality is High by default on every device. *(PERF1)*
- "Balanced" uses the same DPR as High, so it saves no pixels on phones.
- The 929 kB three.js chunk also carries the path tracer and its BVH, so every first 3D view downloads them. *(PERF2)*
- Wipe compare runs two full WebGL views. *(PERF5)*
- The canvas keeps its own MSAA buffer under the High composer, which wastes memory.
- The Welcome screen loads every mode panel. *(PERF4)*
- Not a problem: shadow maps are static, materials are shared, and the frame loop is on demand. *(PERF8 refuted)*

## Cuts
- The Glass look and its setting.
- The top-bar `?` button. The `?` key, the palette and the house menu still open the shortcuts.
- The dock's Compare popover. Compare joins As listed / Proposed as a third segment.
- The floating Measure button. Measure moves into the dock.
- Phone only:
  - editing in Build, Electrics, Garden and Plot
  - the plan-image controls, Keyboard shortcuts, the palette button and Canvas Quiet/Full
  - the High and Balanced quality choices (Auto or Low only)
- The listing image no longer shows by default; one tap on the rail turns it on.
- Coach step 1, the "R" hint in 3D, and the calibration and stairs rows in 3D.
- Dead code: `lib/image.ts`, 11 exports and 6 CSS selectors.

## Proposals for the owner (not built)
1. **A lean phone shell:** no top bar and no tab bar. A floating top-left chip holds the menu, undo and layout, and one dock holds a mode chip (View, Decorate, Furnish). Clear canvas goes from 67 % to 83 %, and landscape works. *(PH1, PH9)*
2. **Five modes:** Plot folds into Garden as "Outside", Review becomes a top-bar button, and Plan/3D moves to the top bar. *(UX11)*
3. Select furniture and electrical points in 3D. *(UX10)*
4. One WebGL canvas for every compare mode, using a scissor at the wipe line. *(PERF5)*
5. Sync images by content hash, so each edit stops uploading the whole project. *(PERF9)*
6. Engineering backlog:
   - ESLint plus typechecking for `server/` and `scripts/` *(CODE2)*
   - `planningCheck` as a table of rules *(CODE13)*
   - split SiteEditor *(CODE7)*
   - move the fixture catalogue into `lib` *(CODE8)*
   - a 6-step type scale *(VIS12)*
7. A real house render behind the Welcome cards. *(VIS13)*
