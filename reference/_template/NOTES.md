# <Display name>: reference notes

This file lives in `src/houses/<id>/NOTES.md` (not in `reference/<id>/`) and is committed with the house.

Every fact the model uses, and where it came from. Cite a photo by its file name without the extension (front-2, kitchen-1) and a measurement as value plus how it was got: "printed on the floorplan", "scaled from the map at 12 px/m", "fitted to front-1", "owner says". Never put an address, postcode, title number or a person's name here; the folder id is the only name.

## Sources
One line per file used (floorplan, map, each photo set) and where it came from (agent, searches, owner's own photos), plus the date.

## Floorplan and scale
Image size in pixels, and the pixels-per-metre used.

## Scale
The method and the spread. Printed room sizes are inner faces (wall to wall); the tracing is on wall centre lines. List the rooms measured between inner wall faces, the px/m each gave, and the spread; then the check against the printed total area and the overall width if printed (printed total versus the model's, as a percentage). In 1989, the map's size of the house against the floorplan's. Any room whose printed size is plainly wrong, and what was done about it.

## Rooms
Per floor, any room whose outline is a judgement call; printed sizes compared with the traced areas, and any difference over a few per cent explained.

## Openings
Doors and windows that are not as the floorplan draws them (swing, width, position), each with the photo or answer that settled it.

## Stairs
Run, direction, number of treads and landings, and the photo or measurement behind them.

## Storey heights
Ground and first floor ceiling heights, floor thickness, and how each was got (stated, counted from brickwork, fitted to a photo).

## Roof
Form (hip, gable, flat), pitch, eaves, chimney, dormers, and the photos each comes from; the attached neighbour's roof if shared.

## Exterior finishes
Wall material and colour, windows, doors, fascias and gutters, per face, with the photo that shows each.

## Interior finishes
Floors, walls, skirting, kitchen and bathroom fittings, radiators, per room, with the photo (fidelity pass only; empty until then).

## Plot and neighbours
Boundary corners, boundary types and heights (wall, fence, hedge), drive and paths, neighbours' footprints, each with its source and the map's accuracy.

## Orientation and location
The front's compass bearing and how it was got, latitude and longitude (rounded to about a kilometre), anything the sun study depends on.

## Open questions
What is still a guess or unanswered, and who could settle it.
